<h3><strong>Important: ECOS Access Changes for IMH &amp; NSC Users</strong></h3>
<p>Our ECOS system vendor will be migrating all IMH &amp; NSC user accounts
to the new @<a rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">nhghealth.com.sg</a> domain.
The transit is expected to complete by 18 August.</p>
<p>Over the past few weeks, DSRB has been actively working to finalise outstanding
IMH &amp; NSC research tasks in preparation for the upcoming system transition.</p>
<p>From 1 August 2025, you can no longer access your existing ECOS studies
using your (old) IMH &amp; NSC ECOS user account. During this period, we
will work closely with the your CRU to support urgent submissions. To ensure
the transition proceeds smoothly, we seek your understanding that access
to ECOS should be on a need to basis.</p>
<p></p>
<h4><strong>Key Dates and Changes</strong></h4>
<p><strong>31 July 2025 (Last day to use existing IMH &amp; NSC ECOS account)</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Complete all pending transactions if possible before end of business day</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Recommended: Document all your ongoing study numbers, including:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Draft submissions</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Studies pending response</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Study renewal requests</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Other outstanding tasks</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
<p></p>
<p><strong>1-17 August 2025 (Transition Period)</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Existing ECOS accounts will no longer be accessible</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>System administrators will migrate all studies and tasks to new @<a rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">nhghealth.com.sg</a> accounts</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Please avoid logging in with new @<a rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">nhghealth.com.sg</a> accounts
during this period</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>For urgent matters, contact your CRU Staff for assistance</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p></p>
<p><strong>From 18 August 2025</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Begin using your new @<a rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">nhghealth.com.sg</a> account
for all ECOS activities</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>All user roles and access permissions will remain unchanged</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Verify that you can access all your assigned studies and tasks.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Report any access issues to ECOS Support.</p>
<p></p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>We truly appreciate the understanding and help from the IMH &amp; NSC
CRU to work through this transit.</p>
<p>We acknowledge there will be inconveniences and if there are other areas
we can support, please let us know at <a rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">OHRPP@nhg.com.sg</a>
</p>

</p>
