<p>Please note there will be ECOS access changes between 15-18 August 2025
due to the OneNHG Email Domain Change effort.</p>
<p><strong>ECOS Access Changes</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>From 15 August, you can no longer access your existing ECOS studies using
your (old) ECOS user account.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Our ECOS system vendor will be migrating your ECOS user accounts to the
new @<a rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">nhghealth.com.sg</a>&nbsp;domain.
This transit is expected to complete by 18 August.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>From 15-17 August (Fri-Sun), please avoid login to ECOS to ensure a smooth
transition.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>On 18 August, you may access ECOS using your (new) NHG Health email.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Key Dates and Changes</strong>
</p>
<p><strong>14 August 2025 (Last day to use existing NHG Group Office and PopHealth Users ECOS account)</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Complete all pending transactions if possible before, 14 August, 6pm</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>For applications planned for September 2025 Full Board review: ensure
endorsements are fully obtained by 14 August</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Recommended: Document all your ongoing study numbers, including:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Draft submissions</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Studies pending response</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Study renewal requests</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Other outstanding tasks</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>15-17 August 2025 (Transition Period)</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Existing ECOS accounts will no longer be accessible</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>System administrators will migrate all studies and tasks to new @<a rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">nhghealth.com.sg</a>&nbsp;accounts</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Please <u>avoid </u>logging in with new @<a rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">nhghealth.com.sg</a>&nbsp;accounts
during this period</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>For <u>urgent</u> safety or non-compliance matters, reach the DSRB Secretariat
for assistance</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>From 18 August 2025 (Monday)</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Begin using your new @<a rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">nhghealth.com.sg</a>&nbsp;account
for all ECOS activities</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>All user roles and access permissions will remain unchanged</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Verify that you can access all your assigned studies and tasks</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Report any access issues to ECOS Support</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>We truly appreciate the understanding and help from you to work through
this transit.</p>
<p>We acknowledge there will be inconveniences and if there are other areas
we can support, please let us know at&nbsp;<a rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">OHRPP@nhg.com.sg</a>
</p>

</p>
